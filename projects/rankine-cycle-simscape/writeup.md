## Overview

Textbook Rankine cycle analysis is steady-state. This project builds a dynamic model to see how pressures, flows and power respond over time, and to check the system settles to a stable operating point.

## What I did

- Developed a dynamic closed-loop Rankine cycle model in MATLAB Simscape (Thermal Liquid library), using thermal ports and controlled heat flow sources.
- Simulated transient pressure and mass flow behaviour across the pump, boiler, turbine and condenser, and evaluated the temperature distribution and mechanical power output to verify system stability.
