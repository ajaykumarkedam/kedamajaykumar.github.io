## Overview

Electric vehicles can brake with the motor (recovering energy) or with friction brakes. This project designs the logic that splits braking torque between the two, including one-pedal driving.

## What I did

- Developed a regenerative/friction torque-split braking strategy with one-pedal driving on an STM32 (Cortex-M4).
- Validated the controller through hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing.
