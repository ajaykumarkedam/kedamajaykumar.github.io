Eigenvalues of the 8-state linear model (roll, steer, four speeds, two lagged tyre forces) at each speed, with modes tracked across speed. The model reproduces the Meijaard et al. (2007) benchmark bicycle in its rigid-tyre limit. Baseline: rake 26°, trail 100 mm, relaxation length 0.20 m, steering damping 1 N·m·s/rad. Several of these are estimates, so treat the critical speeds as approximate. The dashed line marks the published top speed of 115 km/h.
