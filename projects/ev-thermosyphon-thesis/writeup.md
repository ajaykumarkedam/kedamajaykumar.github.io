## Overview

A closed-loop two-phase thermosyphon moves heat without a pump: liquid boils in the evaporator, vapour rises, condenses and returns by gravity. This thesis designs one for the high heat fluxes found in electric-vehicle power electronics and batteries.

## What I did

- Designing a high heat flux closed-loop two-phase thermosyphon consisting of an evaporator, riser, downcomer, and a water-cooled brazed plate heat exchanger as the condenser.
- Investigating the effect of evaporator surface texturing on bubble dynamics, flow regime transition, and boiling heat transfer enhancement under high heat flux conditions.
