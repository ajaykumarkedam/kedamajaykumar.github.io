## Overview

A low-cost unit for homes with unreliable power: it charges from a solar panel or the mains and powers a fan, a study lamp and a phone charger.

## What I did

- Built a 10 V / 5 W solar–mains hybrid with an SG3524-based 50 W sine wave inverter.
- Provided 7805/7812 regulated outputs, giving 8-hour backup for a 4 W LED lamp.
